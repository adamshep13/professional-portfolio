My background

Before joining the Army, I worked in residential and commercial construction and maintenance, including foreman responsibilities. That meant coordinating people, materials, schedules, safety requirements, and the technical details necessary to finish a job correctly.

In the U.S. Army, I became an airborne-qualified parachute rigger and noncommissioned officer. I supported demanding operations, including work with the 10th Special Forces Group. Inspecting, maintaining, and overseeing life-safety equipment required technical precision, familiarity with extensive procedures and manuals, and accountability for the people depending on our work.

After medically retiring from the Army, I continued in commercial transportation and logistics. Those jobs gave me a close view of how scheduling, inventory, equipment, and communication problems affect daily operations. That experience now informs the software I design.

Today, I am completing my B.S. in Computer Information Systems at Saint Louis University and applying my experience to programming, database systems, analytics, cybersecurity, and technology leadership.

PROJECT 03 // ACTIVE CIS CAPSTONE

Dispatch, Inventory & Route Scheduling

My ongoing capstone addresses problems I observed in real delivery operations: fragmented dispatch information, limited inventory visibility, truck loading constraints, and delivery appointments that must be honored within specific time windows.

Using Python, FastAPI, SQLite, and a browser-based interface, I am developing a representative prototype centered on a dispatch dashboard, inventory and vehicle load calculations, and basic route scheduling. The prototype is intentionally separate from live Acumatica data and is being built in stages.
